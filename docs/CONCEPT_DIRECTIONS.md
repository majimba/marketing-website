## Context

The first concept pass explored three premium homepage directions for the Luminary Co. v2 site. These are directional overview concepts only. They are not yet implementation specs.

Before coding, the chosen direction still needs section-by-section concepts for:

1. Hero
2. Problem / category gap
3. Creative Intelligence
4. Build > Create > Amplify
5. Cultural properties / work
6. Final CTA

## Concept A: Editorial Consultancy

Direction:

- stark, typographic, gallery-like
- black/white/gold restraint
- closest to premium brand consultancy / design studio energy

Strengths:

- strongest editorial discipline
- confident typography
- clear premium shift away from the old site
- restrained gold usage
- simple to implement faithfully

Risks:

- could feel slightly too cold or detached if used alone
- needs more cultural warmth and human presence
- less distinctive as a Creative Intelligence method unless we add a stronger system/method moment

Best use:

- typography system
- spacing discipline
- editorial work index
- restraint and polish

## Concept B: Cinematic Cultural Intelligence

Direction:

- filmic, human, atmospheric
- high-contrast cultural photography
- premium cultural studio energy

Strengths:

- strongest emotional resonance
- best sense of African cultural intelligence
- most alive visually
- strong contrast against the old generic site

Risks:

- could become too image-dependent
- needs tight copy and system structure so it does not feel like a film portfolio only
- implementation quality depends on having strong image assets

Best use:

- hero imagery
- cultural properties section
- image treatment
- cinematic pacing

## Concept C: Method-Led Creative Intelligence

Direction:

- strategy/design/innovation consultancy
- the operating method becomes the memorable visual system
- uses light, lens, and sequence motifs

Strengths:

- strongest expression of Creative Intelligence
- best Build > Create > Amplify treatment
- most ownable as a Luminary operating system
- clear bridge between strategy and story
- visually premium without relying only on photography

Risks:

- could become too abstract or consultancy-heavy if not balanced with human/cultural imagery
- needs careful implementation so the method diagram feels elegant, not like a dashboard

Best use:

- overall site structure
- hero method motif
- Creative Intelligence explanation
- Build > Create > Amplify interaction

## Recommended Direction

Proceed with a hybrid:

**Concept C as the structural spine + Concept B as the cinematic soul + Concept A as the editorial discipline.**

That means:

- Use Concept C for the hero architecture and method-led story.
- Use Concept B for cinematic image treatment and cultural atmosphere.
- Use Concept A for typography restraint, spacing, and gallery-like polish.
